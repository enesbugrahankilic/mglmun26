Who we are

A conference built on preparation, not performance.

MGLMUN’26 brings students together to practise diplomacy the way it is really done: reading the files, writing position papers, building coalitions and turning debate into agreed text.

What happens in committee

Opening speeches, moderated and unmoderated caucuses, working papers and a final draft that the committee votes on.

How it works

From application to the final vote

  1. Apply

    Fill in the delegate form with three committee preferences and a short motivation letter.

  2. Get your allocation

    The academic team reviews applications and emails your committee and country.

  3. Prepare

    Read the study guide and write a one-to-two page position paper for your delegation.

  4. Take the floor

    Three days of debate, caucusing and drafting, ending with a vote on your committee’s outcome.

FAQ

Questions before you apply

I have never been to a MUN. Can I apply?

Yes. Write “None” under previous experience. The study guides explain the agenda, and chairs help first-time delegates during the sessions.

How are countries allocated?

The academic team allocates countries and roles after reviewing applications, using your three committee preferences and your motivation letter.

What is a position paper?

A one-to-two page document setting out your delegation’s view on the agenda item and the solutions it supports. The submission deadline will be announced by the Secretariat.

What language is used?

All committees work in English.

Can I still apply for the board or the organisation team?

No. All board, crisis team and organisation team positions have been filled. Delegate applications are still open.

How is my personal data used?

Your data is processed only to organise MGLMUN’26, under Law No. 6698 (KVKK). Photo and video consent and stakeholder sharing are optional.
